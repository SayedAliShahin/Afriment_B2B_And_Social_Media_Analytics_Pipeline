Data Cleaning & Quality Gate Protocol (Excel / Sheets Workflow)Because all data transformations were executed in Excel/Google Sheets rather than Python, our quality control relied on a strict, mandatory 15-minute Quality Gate checklist applied to every data batch before it entered any master tab.   To ensure zero data corruption across our multi-workstream pipeline, every row passed through the following validation checks:   1. Eliminating Trailing Spaces (=TRIM()): Hidden trailing spaces are the single most common reason two lists fail to match or join properly. We enforced a uniform run of the =TRIM() function across all text columns.   2. Fixing Numbers Stored as Text: Left-aligned numbers in Excel silently break downstream calculations and sums. We forced numeric formatting and validated format types at the source.   3. Standardizing Date Formats (YYYY-MM-DD): To prevent Excel from auto-scrambling dates or throwing error codes (such as random 1899 serial numbers), all date fields were standardized to strict YYYY-MM-DD compliance.   4. Preventing Inconsistent Categories (Dropdown Enforcement): Variations like "Data Analytics", "data analytics", and "Data Analysis" register as distinct values to a computer. We eliminated free-text typing entirely by implementing strict Excel Data Validation dropdown lists for categories like Content Pillars, Media Formats, and ICP Match.   5. Duplicate Key Normalization: Rather than checking display names, duplicate checks were performed on normalized keys (e.g., lowering website domains with www. stripped, or matching exact emails for contacts).   6. Provenance & Link Auditing: Every batch required complete provenance tracking (Collected_By, Date_Collected, and Evidence_URL). We tested random samples of 10 links per batch to ensure zero dead links existed from stale research.
