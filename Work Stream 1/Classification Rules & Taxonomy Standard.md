# Classification Rules & Taxonomy Standard
**Author:** Pod B

To prevent inconsistent categories (e.g., treating "Data Analytics", "data analytics", and "Data Analysis" as separate values), all classifications were standardized via strict rules:

## 1. Media Format Definitions
* **Text only:**	No image, video, document or poll attached — caption text alone.
* **Single image:**	One static image attached.
* **Carousel:** Multi-page PDF document upload or native LinkedIn multi-image carousel post.
* **Video:** Native video upload or external link embed featuring moving visual media.
* **Document:**	A PDF or slide deck attached (not classified as Carousel by LinkedIn's own UI).
* **Repost:**	This account resharing someone else's original post.
* **Event:** A LinkedIn Event card attached

## 2. Content Pillar Taxonomy
* **Cohort Celebration/Updates:** Announces or celebrates a cohort starting, graduating, or a milestone within a cohort.
* **Student Testimonial:**	Centers a named learner's story, quote, or outcome in their own words.
* **Educational:**	Teaches a concept, skill, or fact with no direct pitch.
* **Career Advice:**	General guidance on careers, job-hunting, or skill-building not tied to a specific cohort.
* **Recruitment or Enrolment Push:**	Direct call to apply/join a cohort, with urgency language.
* **Company News/Celebration/Announcement:**	Afriment's own milestones, partnerships, or product news (not about a cohort).
* **Industry Commentary:**	Opinion or observation about the training/hiring industry generally.
* **Behind the Scenes:**	Spotlights the Afriment team itself — birthdays, team members, culture.
* **Event Promotion:**	Promotes a specific dated event, webinar, or observance-day activity.
* **Cultural or Holiday Greeting:**	NOT in the brief's original list — added because real data has recurring holiday/cultural posts (New Year, Eid) with no pitch at all.
* **Talent Pool Pitch (B2B):**	NOT in the brief's original list — added because a distinct set of posts pitch companies/employers, not learners.
* **Unclear:**	Doesn't cleanly fit any pillar above, or blends two evenly.
