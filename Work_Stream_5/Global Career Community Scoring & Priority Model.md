# Workstream 5: Global Career Community Scoring & Priority Model (`02_Scoring_Rules.md`)
**Role:** Operational Data Analyst / Pod B Engineering Lead (Afriment Data Analytics Internship)

---

## 1. Overview & Purpose
To evaluate and prioritize global career communities for co-hosting events and partnerships, Pod B applied a weighted scoring model[cite: 5]. This model ranks communities based on engagement effort, audience fit, and activity recency to determine Tier 1 partnership targets[cite: 5].

---

## 2. Priority & Effort Banding
Community prioritization is evaluated through combined criteria:
* **Effort Level:** Categorized as *Low*, *Medium*, or *High* based on operational resources required to execute a joint event[cite: 5].
* **Fit Score:** Calculated by Pod B to measure target alignment and engagement potential[cite: 5].
* **Final Priority:** Communities scoring into **Tier 1** immediately trigger a specific, non-generic co-host idea and a one-page partnership outline[cite: 5].

---

## 3. Mandatory Filters (The Non-Negotiable Gate)
Before scoring, every community must pass two strict validation checks:
1. **The "No Course" Filter:** If a community sells paid training as its core business, it is classified as a competitor and rejected immediately[cite: 1].
2. **The 90-Day Activity Filter:** Every entry must feature a verified activity link proving engagement within the last 90 days[cite: 1].
